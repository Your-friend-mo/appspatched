Not all routes are created equal in Bus Simulator Ultimate. Focus on routes that offer the best profit-to-time ratio rather than simply choosing the longest or most exotic destinations. Master the Driving Mechanics

Smooth driving isn't just about realism – it directly impacts your profit margins through reduced fuel consumption and lower maintenance costs. Avoid sudden acceleration, harsh braking, and excessive speeding, as these behaviors increase wear on your buses and reduce passenger satisfaction scores. Resist the temptation to buy every available bus immediately. Instead, focus on purchasing vehicles that complement your most profitable routes. Luxury coaches work best for long-distance travel, while city buses are perfect for short urban routes. Maximize Passive Income

Hiring AI drivers allows you to earn money from multiple routes simultaneously, but driver quality varies significantly. Invest in skilled drivers even though they cost more upfront – their higher efficiency and lower accident rates provide better long-term returns than cheap alternatives.
