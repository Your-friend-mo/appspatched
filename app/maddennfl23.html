Focus on building a balanced roster rather than spending all your resources on a few superstar players. Strategic Gameplay Fundamentals

Learn to read defensive formations before selecting your plays, as this knowledge will dramatically improve your offensive success rate. If you notice the defense is showing blitz coverage, audible to quick passing routes or running plays that attack the edges. User-controlled defenders can make game-changing plays, so practice manually controlling linebackers or safeties to create interceptions and tackles for loss.
