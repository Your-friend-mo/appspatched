Ball Selection and Equipment Strategy

Your choice of golf ball dramatically impacts your performance in different situations. Power balls excel on courses with long distances but can be difficult to control on precision shots near the hole. Accuracy balls provide better control for tricky shots around obstacles but might leave you short on courses requiring maximum distance. Wind resistance balls become essential on courses with strong wind effects, while bounce balls can help you navigate courses with lots of walls and barriers. Always match your ball selection to the course type you're playing.
