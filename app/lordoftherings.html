Focus on Hero Synergies

Rather than upgrading your highest-rarity heroes randomly, focus on building teams with strong synergies. Fellowship heroes like Frodo, Sam, Merry, and Pippin have powerful bonuses when used together, while Rohan warriors gain significant stat boosts when fighting alongside each other. Reading hero descriptions carefully reveals these hidden synergies that can dramatically increase your team's effectiveness.

Master the Equipment System

Equipment enhancement provides some of the biggest power boosts in the game, often more impactful than hero levels themselves. Prioritize enhancing equipment for your main damage dealers first, focusing on weapons that increase attack power. Don't neglect defensive equipment for your tanks and support heroes, as survivability often determines battle outcomes in challenging content.
