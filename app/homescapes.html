Homescapes Tips and Tricks for Beginners

Master the Basic Combos First

Before diving into complex strategies, focus on consistently creating basic power-ups. Matching four pieces creates directional rockets that clear entire rows or columns, while five pieces in an L or T shape create bombs that clear surrounding areas. The most powerful combo is matching five pieces in a line to create the rainbow ball, which eliminates all pieces of whatever color you swap it with. Practice recognizing these opportunities quickly because they're your primary tools for clearing difficult levels.

Plan Your Moves Around Obstacles

Later levels introduce chains, boxes, and other obstacles that require specific strategies. Chains need to be included in matches to break them, while boxes require adjacent matches or direct hits from power-ups. Always identify the most restrictive obstacles first and plan your moves to clear them efficiently. Don't waste power-ups on easy targets when harder obstacles block your progress.

Save Power-ups for Genuinely Difficult Levels

The game tempts you to use Coins on power-ups for every slightly challenging level, but this quickly drains your resources. Learn to identify which levels actually require boosters versus those you can complete with patience and strategy. Generally, save your Coins for levels with very limited moves, multiple obstacle types, or collection requirements that seem impossible with standard gameplay.

Focus on Creating Combos Over Individual Matches

Combining two power-ups creates devastating effects that can clear entire sections of the board. A rocket combined with a bomb clears three full lines, while two bombs create a massive explosion. The rainbow ball combined with any power-up transforms all pieces of that color into the same power-up type. These combos are essential for completing the hardest levels efficiently.
