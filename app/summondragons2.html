One of the biggest mistakes new players make is focusing solely on individual dragon power ratings. The key to success in Summon Dragons 2 is building teams where dragons complement each other's abilities. Look for dragons with synergistic skills – for example, one dragon might increase fire damage while another deals massive fire-based attacks. A well-balanced team of lower-level dragons with good synergy will often outperform a team of high-level dragons with conflicting abilities.

Prioritize Resource Management

Managing your Gems and Coins efficiently is crucial for long-term success. Don't spend premium resources on the first shiny dragon you see. Instead, save up for guaranteed legendary summon events where you know you'll get powerful dragons. Some dragons have multiple evolution paths, and choosing the wrong one can waste valuable materials. Research the final evolved forms and their abilities before committing to an evolution path. Generally, prioritize evolving dragons that fit your team composition and strategy.

Participate in Daily Events

Daily and weekly events are goldmines for free resources and rare dragons. Even if you can't complete all event objectives, participating gives you valuable rewards. Set reminders for special events and plan your gameplay around maximizing event rewards.
