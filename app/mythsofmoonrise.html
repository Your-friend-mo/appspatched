Myths Of Moonrise Tips and Tricks for Beginners

Early Game Resource Management

Focus on upgrading your resource production buildings before military structures during the first few days. Food and wood generation determine how quickly you can train troops and construct new buildings, making them more valuable than immediate military power. Prioritize lumber mills and farms to level 10 before heavily investing in barracks or defensive structures, as this foundation supports faster long-term growth.

Hero Development Strategy

Don't spread your hero development resources too thin across multiple characters early on. Choose 2-3 heroes that complement each other's abilities and focus all enhancement materials on them until they reach significant power levels. A few strong heroes outperform many weak ones in both PvE content and PvP battles, so concentrate your efforts for maximum impact rather than trying to develop an entire roster simultaneously.

Guild Participation Benefits

Joining an active guild should be your first priority after completing the initial tutorial stages. Guild membership provides daily rewards, exclusive research bonuses, and access to powerful group activities that solo players cannot access. Participate in guild events consistently, even if your contribution seems small – the collective rewards often include rare heroes and substantial resource packages that accelerate your progression significantly.

Research Tree Optimization

The research system offers numerous upgrade paths, but focusing on economic improvements first provides compound benefits throughout your entire gameplay experience. Prioritize research that increases resource production rates, reduces construction times, and enhances troop training efficiency. Military research becomes more important after establishing a solid economic foundation that can support continuous army replacement and expansion.

Battle Formation Tactics

Understanding unit positioning and hero placement determines victory in challenging battles more than raw power levels. Place tanky heroes in front positions to absorb damage while positioning ranged attackers and support characters in protected rear slots. Experiment with different formations against various enemy types, as the optimal setup changes dramatically depending on whether you're facing heavy cavalry, archer-heavy forces, or magic-based opponents.
