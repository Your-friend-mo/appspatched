Your first priority should be building a cohesive 20-card deck rather than stuffing every powerful card you own into a single pile. Learn Proper Resource Management

Timing is everything in Yu-Gi-Oh! Duel Links, and knowing when to commit resources versus when to hold back separates good players from great ones. Similarly, learn to recognize when your opponent is setting up for a big play and save your disruption cards like Floodgate Trap Hole or Canadia for maximum impact rather than using them on the first monster your opponent summons.
