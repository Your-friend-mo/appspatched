One of the biggest mistakes new players make is trying to upgrade every mushroom they collect equally. Instead, identify your strongest 4-5 mushrooms early on and concentrate all your resources on maxing them out. A small team of highly upgraded mushrooms will carry you much further than a large collection of mediocre ones. Pay attention to synergies between mushroom types and build your core team around complementary abilities rather than just choosing the ones with the highest base stats.

Equipment makes an enormous difference in Legend Of Mushroom, often providing more significant power boosts than mushroom upgrades themselves.
