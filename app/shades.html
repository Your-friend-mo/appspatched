The dodge roll isn't just about avoiding damage – it has invincibility frames that make you completely immune to attacks for a brief moment. Learning to time your rolls just as enemy attacks connect will save your life countless times. Practice this timing against weaker enemies before attempting challenging boss fights. The visual cue is usually the enemy's weapon starting to glow or their animation reaching the peak of the swing.

Prioritize Weapon Synergies Over Raw Damage

Don't just grab the weapon with the highest damage number. Look for weapons that complement your playstyle and work well with your current abilities. A fast dagger that triggers your poison ability on every hit might be more valuable than a slow sword with higher base damage. Experiment with different combinations to find synergies that multiply your effectiveness.

Learn Enemy Attack Patterns

Every enemy type has specific attack patterns and tells that telegraph their moves. Shadow warriors often pause before heavy attacks, giving you time to dodge or counter. Ranged enemies typically have predictable firing sequences. Spend time observing enemy behavior rather than button-mashing – patience and pattern recognition will take you further than raw reflexes.

Manage Your Abilities Strategically

Don't waste powerful abilities on weak enemies just because they're available. Save your high-damage or crowd-control abilities for dangerous situations: tough mini-bosses, overwhelming enemy groups, or when your health is critically low.
