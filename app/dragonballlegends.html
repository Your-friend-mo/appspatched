Dragon Ball Legends Tips and Tricks for Beginners

Master the Combat System

Dragon Ball Legends combat is all about timing and card management. Don't just spam your arts cards randomly – learn to chain them together for maximum damage. Strike cards build up your ki gauge faster, while blast cards deal more damage from a distance. Vanishing Step is your best friend – master the timing to dodge enemy attacks and immediately counter with your own combo. Always keep an eye on your opponent's vanish gauge; when it's empty, that's your chance to unleash a devastating combo they can't escape from.

Build Balanced Teams

Team synergy is everything in Dragon Ball Legends. Don't just throw your highest-power characters together and expect to dominate. Focus on building teams around specific tags like "Saiyans," "God Ki," or "Future" to maximize your Z-abilities and stat boosts. Each character provides different buffs to teammates, so read their Z-abilities carefully and plan accordingly. Having three core fighters and three bench support units with matching tags will give you a massive advantage over players who ignore team building.

Manage Your Resources Wisely

Even with unlimited Chrono Crystals from our generator, it's important to be strategic about when and where you spend them. Focus on Step-Up banners and Legendary Limited banners that offer guaranteed featured characters or special rates. Don't waste crystals on regular banners unless you're specifically targeting a character you need. Also, prioritize upgrading your main team's soul boost panels and equipment before spreading resources too thin across multiple characters.

Equipment and Soul Boosting

Many new players overlook equipment, but it's one of the most important aspects of character progression. Focus on getting S and Z-rank equipment for your main fighters, and don't forget to upgrade them using skip tickets and Zeni. Soul boosting is equally important – a fully soul-boosted character at lower stars will often outperform a higher-star character with incomplete soul boost panels. Use your energy efficiently by farming soul boost materials during bonus events when possible.
