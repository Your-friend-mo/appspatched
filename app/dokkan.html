What is Dokkan Battle?

Dragon Ball Z: Dokkan Battle is a free-to-play mobile RPG developed by Akatsuki, combining match-three puzzle mechanics with classic RPG team building and Dragon Ball's legendary characters. Available on both iOS and Android platforms, the game launched in 2015 and has since become one of the highest-grossing mobile games worldwide, consistently ranking in the top charts across multiple countries.

The core gameplay revolves around building teams of up to six characters, each with unique abilities, link skills, and transformation mechanics. Combat occurs on a board where you connect colored Ki spheres to charge your characters' super attacks – the more spheres you connect, the more powerful your attacks become. What makes this system incredibly deep is how different characters synergize through link skills, leader skills, and categories, creating endless team-building possibilities that can dramatically change your battle performance.

The addiction factor comes from Dokkan Battle's gacha system and constant power creep. New banners featuring stronger characters drop regularly, each promising to revolutionize the meta. Events like the World Tournament, Extreme Z-Battles, and story mode provide steady content, while special celebrations bring exclusive units and massive stone bonuses. The progression system extends far beyond just pulling characters – you'll spend countless hours farming medals to awaken units, grinding link levels, and optimizing rotations for maximum damage output.

Understanding leader skills is fundamental to building effective teams in Dokkan Battle. Category leaders typically provide the strongest boosts, but don't overlook type leaders when you're starting out – they're more flexible for building diverse teams with limited character rosters.

Link skills are the secret sauce that separates good teams from exceptional ones.
