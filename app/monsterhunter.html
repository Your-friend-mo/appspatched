What is Monster Hunter Now?

Monster Hunter Now is a location-based augmented reality action RPG developed by Niantic (yes, the Pokemon GO creators) in collaboration with Capcom. Focus on one weapon type initially rather than spreading resources across multiple options. I recommend starting with Sword and Shield for versatility or Great Sword for raw damage output. Once you've mastered your primary weapon's moveset and upgrade path, expanding to secondary options becomes much more manageable and cost-effective.

Location-Based Hunting Strategy

Different areas spawn specific monster types, so plan your hunting routes around target creatures rather than wandering randomly. Urban areas typically feature smaller monsters like Great Jagras, while parks and open spaces spawn larger creatures including Rathalos and Diablos. Use the in-game map to identify monster habitats and schedule your hunting sessions around real-world travel to maximize encounter efficiency.

Resource Management and Storage

Inventory space becomes critically important as you progress, so prioritize storage upgrades early using your Gems wisely. Keep essential items like potions, traps, and crafting materials while selling excess common monster parts for Coins. Create a sustainable cycle where you're constantly upgrading equipment with farmed materials rather than hoarding everything indefinitely.

Multiplayer Cooperation

Join hunting parties whenever possible, especially for challenging monsters that would be difficult solo. Coordinate with other players using the in-game communication tools, and don't hesitate to signal for help when overwhelmed. The social aspects of Monster Hunter Now create lasting friendships while making difficult hunts significantly more manageable and enjoyable.

Daily and Event Optimization

Complete daily quests and participate in limited-time events religiously since these provide the best resource-to-time investment ratios. Event monsters often drop rare materials unavailable through normal hunting, while daily bonuses accumulate substantial rewards for consistent players. Set reminders for event schedules and prioritize these activities over regular grinding sessions.
