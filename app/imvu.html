Smart Shopping Strategies

Always check the "On Sale" section first before making purchases, as IMVU regularly discounts popular items by 25-50%. Join your favorite creators' groups to receive notifications about new releases and exclusive discounts. Consider purchasing outfit bundles when available, as they typically offer better value than buying individual pieces separately. Room Design and Decoration

Focus on lighting first when decorating rooms, as proper lighting can make even budget furniture look expensive and atmospheric. Participate in IMVU's official contests and community challenges, which often provide rewards and recognition that can boost your social standing.
