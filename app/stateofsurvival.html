State Of Survival Tips and Tricks for Beginners

Settlement Development Strategy

Focus on upgrading your Settlement Headquarters as your top priority since it unlocks new buildings and features that are essential for long-term growth. Balance your building upgrades rather than maxing out one structure at a time – having multiple buildings under construction simultaneously maximizes your efficiency. Always keep your builders busy, and don't forget to upgrade resource production buildings regularly to maintain steady supplies for ongoing construction projects.

Resource Management Fundamentals

Never let your resources reach maximum capacity without spending them, as this stops production and wastes potential growth. Prioritize research upgrades that increase resource production rates and storage capacity early in the game. Join an active alliance immediately – alliance help can reduce construction and research times by several hours, dramatically accelerating your overall progress. Use your gas wisely for gathering expeditions to infected zones, as these provide valuable materials and experience points for your survivors.

Combat and Survivor Optimization

Invest in survivor development through training and equipment upgrades rather than just collecting new survivors. A few well-developed survivors with proper gear will outperform a large collection of weak ones. Focus on unlocking and upgrading survivors that complement your preferred gameplay style – whether that's base defense, gathering efficiency, or combat prowess. Don't neglect your infantry, hunter, and rider training facilities, as balanced troop compositions are essential for both PvE missions and PvP encounters.

Alliance Warfare and Social Features

Participate actively in alliance events and donated regularly to alliance technology research, as these provide significant bonuses that benefit your entire gameplay experience. Coordinate with alliance members for rally attacks against stronger infected zones or enemy settlements – teamwork often succeeds where solo attempts fail.
