Combat and Weapon Strategies

Learn the attack patterns of different infected types and practice dodging their special abilities rather than trying to tank damage. Melee weapons are incredibly effective for conserving ammunition, especially when dealing with single enemies or small groups. Always carry backup weapons because durability degradation happens faster than you expect during intense combat situations. Headshots deal significantly more damage to most enemies, so practice your aim and positioning to maximize your damage output.

Exploration and Survival Tactics

Never venture into high-level areas without proper preparation – bring extra food, medical supplies, and repair materials for extended expeditions. Mark important locations on your map, including resource nodes, safe houses, and trader locations that you'll want to revisit regularly. Travel during daylight hours when visibility is better and certain dangerous enemies are less active. Always check your surroundings before engaging in lengthy activities like crafting or base building, as other players might be nearby.
