Understanding how different piece combinations create special effects is fundamental to Royal Match success. The most powerful combination involves matching five pieces, which creates the Royal Hammer that can eliminate any piece on the board. Practice recognizing these patterns quickly, as identifying combination opportunities often determines whether you'll complete a level within the move limit.

Strategic Booster Usage

Resist the temptation to use boosters immediately when you encounter difficulty.
