Evony Tips and Tricks for Beginners

Focus on Resource Production Early

The biggest mistake new players make is rushing to build military structures without establishing a solid economic foundation first. Your farms, sawmills, quarries, and iron mines are the backbone of your empire, and upgrading them should be your absolute top priority during the early game. A strong resource production base will support all your future endeavors, from building massive armies to constructing advanced buildings. Don't get distracted by flashy military units when your economy can't support them long-term.

Join an Active Alliance Immediately

Playing Evony solo is like trying to fight a dragon with a butter knife – technically possible but incredibly inefficient and painful. Active alliances provide crucial benefits including construction help that reduces building times, protection from enemy attacks, shared resources during shortages, and access to alliance-exclusive events and rewards. Look for alliances with members who are online regularly and willing to help newcomers learn the ropes. The social aspect of the game becomes much more enjoyable when you have reliable allies watching your back.

Research Technology Strategically

The research tree in Evony can seem overwhelming at first, but focusing on the right technologies early will give you massive advantages over players who research randomly. Prioritize economic research that boosts resource production and reduces construction times, then move into military research that improves your army's effectiveness. Don't spread your research efforts too thin – it's better to max out a few crucial technologies than to have mediocre progress across everything.

Manage Your Resources Wisely

Resource management separates good players from great ones in Evony. Always keep your resources below your warehouse protection limit when you're offline to prevent other players from raiding them. Use resources immediately for construction or research rather than hoarding them unnecessarily. Learn the market values of different resources so you can make profitable trades with other players and alliances. Smart resource management will keep your empire growing steadily even when you're not actively playing.

Plan Your City Layout Carefully

Your city layout affects both efficiency and defensibility, so don't just place buildings randomly. Keep resource-producing buildings near the center where they're harder for enemies to target during attacks. Position defensive structures strategically to create chokepoints and maximize their effectiveness. Plan ahead for higher-level buildings that require more space, and leave room for expansion rather than cramming everything together early on. A well-planned city layout will serve you well throughout your entire Evony career.
