What is AFK Arena?

AFK Arena is an idle RPG developed by Lilith Games that launched in 2019 and quickly became a global phenomenon. The core gameplay revolves around assembling a team of five heroes who battle automatically through various game modes, earning rewards even when you're not actively playing. What sets it apart from other idle games is the strategic depth – hero positioning, faction synergies, and timing your ultimate abilities can make the difference between victory and defeat.

The game features over 100 unique heroes across seven different factions: Lightbearers, Maulers, Wilders, Graveborn, Celestials, Hypogeans, and Dimensionals. Each hero has distinct abilities, and the faction system creates interesting team-building puzzles where certain combinations unlock powerful bonuses. The AFK Arena progression system is built around ascending your heroes, which requires multiple copies of the same character – this is where the game's gacha mechanics come into play.

Understanding hero positioning makes a massive difference in battle outcomes. Your strongest tank should usually go in the front center position, while your main carry often works best in the back center spot where they're protected but can still target enemies effectively.
